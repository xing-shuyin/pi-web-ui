The interaction-architecture panorama, one level up Architecture-level comparison

Shape 1: Smart accordion (one project open · many projects, no long scroll)

    💡 Architectural reflection: why does one deep nested tree feel "not quite there"?

    1
    Space explosion: with 25 projects, opening two or three at once makes the sidebar scrollbar a hair-thin sliver — every lookup is a hunt through thousands of pixels;
    2
    Horizontal width tax: nesting always costs left padding and guide lines, so titles are the first thing to get truncated on small screens or in a narrow sidebar;
    3
    The mental-focus argument: at any given moment nearly every developer is deep in exactly one project (the current workspace). Shape 1 and shape 2 obey that law and push the noise to a minimum.
